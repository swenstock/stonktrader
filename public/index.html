How It Works

Start small. Win your way up. Become a Broker.

01

Enter a satellite

Pick any of the daily sessions (Full Day, Morning, Afternoon) or the Weekly Qualifier — each runs at four price points starting around $1, plus a free roll on the Weekly Qualifier. Entering gives you a dedicated $100,000 paper portfolio, just for that contest.

02

Trade real markets

US and global tickers, live prices. Your picks in this portfolio determine your rank in this contest only — trading in one contest never affects any other portfolio you're in.

03

Finish high enough, win a ticket

Every satellite's prize pool funds as many 3,000 STONK Main Event tickets as it can support. Finish inside that cutoff and you get a ticket — a fully funded, free entry into the Main Event.

04

Play your ticket in the Main Event

Redeem your ticket for a free seat in this week's Main Event — no extra charge. You're now playing for a shot at a real Activated Stonk Broker.

05

The Main Event pays out in Brokers

The Main Event's prize pool funds as many Activated Stonk Brokers as it can support — could be one, could be several. Whatever's left over goes winner-take-all in STONK to the next finisher.

06

You can stack contests

Nothing stops you from being in the Weekly Qualifier, a Morning session, and a Full Day session all at once — each has its own portfolio and its own shot at a ticket.

Rules

This is a stock-picking contest, not a casino. We build the rules to reward it.

The whole point of this game is proving you can pick stocks — not proving you can hit "max buy" on one ticker thirty seconds before the bell and hope. A portfolio that goes all-in on a single YOLO trade right before a session locks isn't skill, it's a coin flip wearing a trading costume. The rules below exist to keep the contest about picking, sizing, and managing a real portfolio — the same instincts that matter in real markets.

RULE 01

$2B minimum market cap

Every tradable symbol on the platform is a large, established company. No micro-caps, no penny stocks, no thinly-traded names that move 40% on a rumor. If a stock isn't listed here, it's below the bar — that's intentional.

RULE 02

Max 10% of your portfolio per position — at entry

Any single buy that would push a position's cost basis over 10% of your portfolio's current value gets blocked. This applies only to new purchases — if a position you already own grows past 10% because the stock ran up, that's completely fine and not restricted. The rule stops you from betting the whole portfolio on one name; it doesn't punish you for being right.

WHY

No YOLO trades near the bell

Dumping your entire portfolio into one position in the last few minutes before a contest locks isn't trading skill, it's a lottery ticket — and it makes every leaderboard about luck instead of picks. Sizing limits keep every entrant's result reflecting a real, diversified set of decisions made across the whole session, not one desperate swing at the buzzer.
